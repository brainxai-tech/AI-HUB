# Read Research Paper

1. Import a supported link or parse pasted text.
2. Preserve section and paragraph IDs, summaries, and citation labels.
3. Generate the paper map from bounded source context.
4. Pause for questions or study tasks.
5. Retrieve relevant paragraphs locally before every explanation, answer, or quiz.
6. Pass retrieved citations to the paper coach and retain its evidence labels.
7. Finish only when the user explicitly closes the reading session.

Do not turn an inference into a paper claim. State when the imported text is incomplete, a PDF lacks a text layer, or no supporting paragraph was retrieved.
